# Sakura Hearts 🌸

An open-world pixel-art anime life sim for the browser, in the spirit of Stardew Valley and
Animal Crossing. You've just moved to the seaside town of Sakuragaoka. Walk around town, work,
study and train, and grow your relationship with the nine girls you meet until they become your girlfriends (one, a few, or all of them).

**Play online:** https://claude.ai/artifact/JF3AHasmgfN29ENbEDXZ1K
(or open `index.html` in any modern browser; no build step, no dependencies)

## The girls

Each girl has her own daily routine. She walks around town between home, work, school and her
favourite spots, and the routine changes on weekends.

## How it works

- **Open world:** a 90×40 tile town with a shrine, academy, dojo, café, gift shop, arcade, plaza, sakura park, beach and pier, plus an east side with Sakura Onsen, the Clock Tower, Forest Lake, Café Blue Rose (maid café), Honey Bakery, Grimoire Antiques, the Flower Field with its windmill, and Lighthouse Cape. The game fills the screen: touch and drag anywhere to use the floating joystick (or arrow keys / WASD), with the A, Map, Phone and Bag buttons in the corner. ⛶ switches to true fullscreen where the browser allows it. Press **A** (or E / Space) to talk or interact, and walk into a door to go inside.
- **Clock:** time runs while you're outside, from 6am to 2am, with sunsets and lamp-lit nights. Activities, talks and dates also take time. If you're still out at 2am, you pass out.
- **Interiors:** every building is a room you walk around in. Use the furniture: bed, TV forecast and fridge at home, the café counter and menu board, library shelves and study tables, the gift shop counter and fitting mirror, arcade cabinets and claw machine, the dojo's training dummy and taiko drum.
- **Outfits:** everyone wears swimsuits on the beach sand (you too) and towels inside Sakura Onsen. With a girl at Close Friend or higher you can "Soak together" in the hot spring, or take her on a hot spring date.
- **Life mode (Sims-style):** under the clock are your 🍔 hunger, 🚽 bladder, 🧼 hygiene, 🎉 fun and 💗 health. They drain as time passes.
  - **Eating:** cook at home with groceries (from the bakery or gift shop), grab a snack from the fridge, or eat out (café, maid café, bakery, city restaurants).
  - **Toilets:** at home, in every shop (the "Use the restroom" option) and in the blue public restrooms in town and the city. Hold it too long and you have an embarrassing accident.
  - **Hygiene and fun:** low hygiene halves the affection you earn, and being bored halves stat gains.
  - **Health:** starving or exhaustion drains it. Collapse and the clinic bills you ¥150.
  - **Rent:** ¥100 is due every Monday (work at Café Lumière or the maid café).
  - **Game over:** collapse with no money, or miss rent three weeks running. You can retry from that morning or carry on in Pacifist mode.
  - **The girls live too:** each has a job and daily wage, savings, rent, a hobby to pay for, and hunger, energy and fun bars (shown on the Phone). Hungry girls go to eat, exhausted ones go home, broke ones pick up extra shifts, and their mood changes how much affection you earn. Lend them money or buy them a snack when times are hard.
- **Pacifist mode:** choose it when starting a new game, or switch any time in 📱 ⚙️. No needs, rent or game over, so it plays the way the game did before life mode.
- **Multiple girlfriends:** you can date as many of them as you like, up to the whole cast. Confessing while you already have girlfriends gets her own line about sharing you, and two of your girlfriends together have a word about it too.
- **Hanging out in a group:** walk together with one girl, then invite up to two more along ("Invite to hang out"). Every pair of girls has her own unique exchange, which plays as they chat during the walk. Girls standing together around town also chat with speech bubbles, and may pull you into their conversation. Take the whole group on the train to the city for a group date.
- **Date clothes:** everyone has a casual date outfit (Nami's sundress, Aqua's hoodie, Yumeko's red skirt, Frieren's knit sweater, Kurumi's gothic dress, Rem's pastel blouse, Zero Two's red jacket, Marin's crop top, your denim jacket). They're worn on dates and city trips, and the wardrobe's 💃 Date tab can change the style and colour or make them everyday wear.
- **Le Ciel Étoilé:** a fancy French restaurant in the city with a candlelit tasting menu (from 5pm, dress code: date clothes) and a grand piano you can play.
- **Hoshimachi City:** take the train from Sakuragaoka Station (south of the road, by the railway) to the city. It has a busy street with traffic, a plaza with a fountain and a crêpe truck, and the shops:
  - McRonald's (burgers; kids' meals come with a toy)
  - Kentucky Fried Kitsune (fried chicken)
  - Kura-Kura Sushi (conveyor-belt sushi)
  - Hoshimachi Megamall (fashion boutique, purikura photo booth, gift boutique with city-only gifts)
- **City dates:** pick "Hoshimachi City (by train)" when asking her out. You ride the train together, and she follows you into every shop. Eating together, trying on clothes (you can buy the outfit for her) and taking purikura photos are each their own date scene, and she likes some places more than others. Take the train home when you're done, or she'll ask to catch it at 11pm. Purikura photos are kept in your bag.
- **Your house:** a bedroom (bed, nightstand, wardrobe, desk), a living room (TV, sofa, rug), an open kitchen (stove, sink, fridge, dining table) and a bathroom (shower, bathtub, washbasin, toilet, washing machine). Cook a meal, take a shower or a hot bath, or nap on the sofa. Each one plays as an animation. The girls' apartments have their own kitchenette and bathroom.
- **Wardrobe:** use the wardrobe at home (or 📱 Phone → ⚙️ → Wardrobe) to pick your coat colour, beachwear and towel, and each girl's one-piece or bikini, swimsuit colour, towel and pajamas (style, colour, nightcap).
- **Animated scenes:** soaking together, swimming, sunbathing, fishing, training, hugs, hand-holding and more play out in the world with poses, emotes, steam, splashes and hearts.
- **Overnight stays:** from 8pm to 1am, ask a Close Friend to spend the night at your place (she takes the bed, you get the sofa). Once she's your girlfriend you can also stay at hers, in her own bedroom, and you share the bed. You change into pajamas, talk on the rug, sleep, and get a morning scene. Each girl has her own sleepwear: tee and shorts (Nami, Aqua), red silk set (Yumeko), gothic nightgown (Kurumi), polka dots (Rem), oversized shirt (Zero Two), gown and nightcap (Frieren).
- **Flirty tease (Nami and Aqua):** once Nami or Aqua is a serious girlfriend, ask her for a 😉 Flirty tease when you're alone together at your place or hers. If she's in the mood, she does a cheeky twirl that's hidden behind hearts and sparkles; she can also say no, and it's once a day.
- **Love potion:** Frieren sells 🧪 Love Potions (¥120). Give one to any girl for +20 ♥ that ignores the usual date limits.
- **Weather:** sunny, cloudy, rain, thunderstorms (lightning and thunder) and sakura blizzards. Rain sends everyone indoors, a blizzard brings everyone to the park from 3 to 6pm, and the girls react to it. Check tomorrow's forecast on the TV.
- **Run:** tap **RUN** next to the A button (or hold Shift) to run at nearly twice the speed. Girls walking with you keep up.
- **WhatsUp with real typing:** type anything to a girl and she answers in her own voice, all on your device (no internet or AI credits). She understands greetings, "how are you", "what are you doing", compliments, flirting, confessions, insults and apologies, food, work, weather, other girls' names, jokes and your mood. She remembers what you tell her ("my favourite food is ramen", "I like cats", "I'm from Osaka") and can recall it later ("what's my favourite food?"). Asking to meet up sets a real meeting on the map, kind messages raise affection a little each day, and rude ones lower it.
- **Every line her own:** turning you down for an early date, walk or sleepover, saying yes to a date, rainy and sakura dates, birthday hints, crêpes, love potions, small talk and more are written separately for each girl, and the game never repeats the same line twice in a row.
- **Colour every part:** in the 👗 wardrobe, every outfit (daily, date, beach, onsen, night and Marin's cosplays) lists its parts (top, waist, legs, shoes and details) by colour. Tap a part and pick from the palette or any colour you like. Shading is kept, and the walking sprite changes too.
- **Pixel icons:** every emoji in the game has been replaced by a hand-designed 16×16 pixel icon (drawn in code in `js/icons.js`).
- **Video games:** the TV at home has a game console: Kart Dash, Block Stack, Rhythm Hearts and Brawl Mash. Play solo, or versus any girl who is with you (walking together, a sleepover). Each girl has her own skill at each game, and her own win and lose lines.
- **Arcade:** every cabinet is a playable minigame (¥5 a game, ¥10 for two), solo or against the girls you brought.
- **Lucky Seven Casino:** on the far east side (open 4pm–2am). Slot machines (¥10/50/100 bets) and a blackjack table (dealer stands on 17, blackjack pays 3:2). Yumeko is a regular in the evenings.
- **Cards with Yumeko:** "Play cards" changes with where she is: high-stakes blackjack at the casino, small-stakes blackjack at the arcade, high-low in the academy library or anywhere else.
- **Marin's cosplay shoots:** from 2 hearts, ask Marin for a photoshoot. She changes into Shizuku-tan (gothic black-lily maid), Rizu-kyun (little devil with wings and tail) or a magical girl, keeps it on for the rest of the day, and you direct the pose (the right pose for the costume earns extra hearts). The photo goes in your bag. In the wardrobe you can also have her cosplay every day.
- **Work from home:** at your desk, 3 hours of freelance work for ¥40 plus a Smarts bonus.
- **Rich Kurumi:** she lives in Tokisaki Mansion, earns a daily dividend, pays for dates, train tickets and city meals when she's with you, and (from 4½ hearts) gives you ¥300 once a week when you're broke.
- **WhatsUp:** the phone's chat app. Every girl you've met has a chat with bubbles, read ticks and a typing indicator. Send quick replies: say hi (once a day for a heart boost), ask her to meet up later (she picks a place and time), "Thinking of you" for girlfriends, and good night. With two or more girlfriends, there's a group chat too. Her morning invitations and the landlord's reminders arrive here.
- **📖 Walkthrough & guide:** from the title screen or 📱 ⚙️. Basics, every girl's routine, loved gifts and dates, confession needs and tips, life mode, money and the games.
- **Followers come inside:** girls walking with you follow you into every building.
- **Activities:** work at the café, study at the academy, train at the dojo, jog the park course, swim at the beach, make shrine offerings, play at the arcade, shop for gifts.
- **Affection:** talk to each girl once a day, pick replies she likes, give gifts she loves (double on her birthday), and take her on dates. You can also Interact (compliment, joke, tease, head pat, hold hands, hug, kiss) or Walk together, and she'll follow you around town.
- **Relationship events:** morning texts inviting you to meet her somewhere (missing one costs affection), chance run-ins around town where she might give you something, birthdays, and weather moments.
- **Dates gate progress:** affection stalls at 45 until your first date and at 75 until your third. Heart events happen at 35 and 65, and you can confess at 90 if you meet her standards.
- **Saving:** three save slots, and the slot you're playing autosaves all the time. It saves to the browser right away and to your private cloud save (the artifact's database, under `data/users/<you>/slotN`) every ~20 seconds and whenever you leave the page, so progress survives game updates, new sessions and other devices. Load, save to another slot, or delete a slot from the title screen or 📱 Phone → System.
- **📱 Phone** shows each girl's routine, the gifts and date spots she likes that you've found, and your next goal. **🗺️ Map** shows where everyone is right now.

## Code

All pixel art is drawn in code (no image files).

- `js/pixel.js`: tiny per-pixel raster (shapes, mirroring, auto-outline)
- `js/sprites.js`: large chibi portraits (the four girls and the player) with expressions and blinking
- `js/walkers.js`: 16×24 overworld sprites, 4 directions × walk frames
- `js/interiors.js`: the six walk-around building interiors (rooms, furniture, pathfinding)
- `js/world.js`: the town map, buildings, props, trees, collision, A* pathfinding, minimap
- `js/backgrounds.js`: interior and date scenes, lit for each time of day
- `js/icons.js`: the pixel icons that replace emoji in the UI
- `js/chatbot.js`: the offline chat engine for WhatsUp (intents, memory, each girl's voice)
- `js/minigames.js`: the console/arcade minigames, slots, blackjack and high-low
- `js/data.js`: all content: schedules, hours, activities, dialogue, gifts, dates, endings
- `js/game.js`: game loop, clock, girl routines, movement, dialogue, UI, save/load
- `tools/`: dev helpers (sprite/map previews, Playwright play-test bots, single-file build)

Dev: `node tools/update.js <outdir>` checks running, per-girl lines, part colours, typed chat, icons and old saves with Rei;
`node tools/fun.js <outdir>` plays the console, arcade, casino, Yumeko's cards, Marin's cosplay, WhatsUp and the guide;
`node tools/features.js <outdir>` exercises interiors, furniture, weather, interactions, walks and texts;
`node tools/saves.js <outdir>` checks new game → autosave → reload → continue → save to another slot;
`node tools/layout.js <outdir> 390 844 p` screenshots the fullscreen layout at a viewport size;
`node tools/play.js <outdir> 300` walks around with a random bot and reports JS errors;
`node tools/route.js <outdir> aqua 600` jumps one girl's route to the confession and ending;
`node tools/tease.js <outdir>` checks Nami and Aqua's private tease (privacy gate and the scene);
`node tools/build-artifact.js <out.html>` bundles everything into one page.
